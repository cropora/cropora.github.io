Your Privacy Matters

We are committed to protecting your privacy and keeping your farm information secure. This page explains what information Cropora collects, how it is used, and how it is protected.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cropora ("the App", "we", "our", or "us"). We value your privacy and are committed to protecting your information. This Privacy Policy explains how Cropora collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the App.

By using Cropora, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Cropora is designed to help farmers manage labour, fields, crops, expenses, crop sales, and other farming activities.

Cropora does not collect, transmit, or store your personal information on any external server operated by Cropora.

Information you choose to enter into the App may include:

  • Your name (during initial registration)
  • Farm details
  • Labour information
  • Attendance records
  • Salary records
  • Crop information
  • Field information
  • Expense records
  • Sales records
  • Other farming-related information entered by you

All of this information is stored only on your device.

3. Local Storage

All data created within Cropora is stored locally on your Android device using the device's internal storage.

Cropora does not upload your data to cloud storage, external servers, or any online database.

4. User Registration

During the first launch, Cropora asks you to complete a one-time registration to personalize your experience.

Cropora does not require:

  • Login
  • Password
  • Online account
  • User authentication

Registration information remains stored only on your device.

5. App Permissions

Camera Permission

Cropora requests Camera permission only when you choose to capture or upload your profile photo. The camera is never accessed without your permission, and your photos remain stored only on your device. Cropora does not collect or share these photos.

The camera is never accessed without your permission, and captured images remain stored on your device.

Notification Permission

Cropora requests notification permission to send useful reminders, including attendance reminders.

Future versions may also include other farming reminders and important application notifications.

Notifications are generated by the App and are never used for advertising or tracking purposes.

6. PDF Export

Cropora allows you to generate PDF reports for your farming records.

Generated PDF files are saved locally on your device. Cropora does not upload these files to any server.

7. Internet Connection

Most features of Cropora work completely offline.

An internet connection is only required for displaying advertisements through Google AdMob and may also be required for future optional online features.

8. Advertisements

Cropora uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google AdMob, as an advertising service, may collect certain information such as:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • Diagnostic information

This information is collected by Google in accordance with Google's own Privacy Policy and is not controlled by Cropora.

For more information, please visit:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

9. Data Sharing

Cropora does not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties.

The only exception is information that may be collected automatically by Google AdMob for advertising purposes.

10. Your Rights

Since all farming information is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data.

You may:

  • View your records at any time.
  • Edit your records.
  • Delete individual records.
  • Remove all data by clearing the App's storage or uninstalling the App.

11. Data Security

Your farm records remain stored on your own device.

We take reasonable measures to help protect your information by keeping all application data locally rather than transmitting it to external servers.

However, no method of electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Data Deletion

You have complete control over your data.

You can remove your information by:

  • Deleting records within the App.
  • Clearing the App's data from Android Settings.
  • Uninstalling the App.

Once deleted, locally stored data cannot be recovered.

13. Children's Privacy

Cropora is intended for general audiences, including farmers and agricultural workers.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Any updates will be reflected by changing the Last Updated date at the top of this document.

15. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the Cropora App, please contact us.
